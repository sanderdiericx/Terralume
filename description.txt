Terralume completely overhauls vanilla rendering to bring physically based rendering to Terraria. Materials respond to light with richer shading, reflections, and surface detail.

Features include:
- Material-based lighting: Surfaces react differently to light based on their material properties.
- Normal mapping: Surface shading gives flat textures a more detailed, 3D appearance.
- Reflections: Reflective surfaces reflect their surroundings.
- Dynamic lighting: Light sources and sunlight work with the new rendering system which results in more detailed shading.
- Bloom: Bright lights and glowing elements receive a soft glow.
- Armor materials: Supported armor pieces respond to lighting according to their material.
- Volumetric lighting: Lights produce atmospheric halos that interact with the environment.

Terralume is still in active development. Expect bugs, visual glitches, and rough edges while exploring and playing.
Some materials and effects may need further tuning. Bug reports and feedback are welcome and will help improve future releases.

Performance

A fairly capable GPU is required, as the rendering effects are more demanding than vanilla Terraria. 
For reference, the game runs smoothly on my RTX 3060 at 1920×1200. Performance will vary depending on your hardware, resolution, and what is happening on screen.

Mod Compatibility

Terralume makes extensive changes to Terraria’s rendering pipeline and is likely incompatible with other mods that modify lighting or rendering.

Content added by other mods, such as Calamity, requires manually defined material properties to take full advantage of Terralume’s rendering. Content without these definitions currently uses a fallback material.
A system allowing other mods to provide their own material definitions is in development and will be available in a future update.

Known Issues

Terraria’s screen shader effects currently do not work correctly with Terralume enabled.
